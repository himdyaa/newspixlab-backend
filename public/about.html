About Us

NewsPixLab ek subscription-based automated news posting service hai, jo Blogger blog owners ke liye banayi gayi hai.

Hum roz verified Hindi news research karke, likhkar, aur aapke Blogger blog par automatically publish karte hain. Categories me Desh, Videsh, Pradesh, Khel, Manoranjan, Technology, Business aur Dharm-Astha shamil hain.

Clients monthly plans (Rs 2500 – Rs 5000) me se chun sakte hain, apni pasand ki news categories select kar sakte hain, aur Google OAuth ke zariye apna blog securely connect kar sakte hain. Aapka password hum kabhi nahi mangte.

Har news ke saath matching posters bhi taiyar karke client ke Instagram par bheje jate hain.
